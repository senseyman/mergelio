# New

- **Window position.** Mergelio reopens where you left it, not only at the same size. If that monitor is gone or the screen changed, the window is moved onto a connected display or centred. Maximized and full-screen states are not restored.
- **Line history from any diff.** Pick a line or a run of lines, in blame or in any diff, to see the commits that changed just those lines. Each commit shows what it did to them. Click a row to open that commit's full diff.
- **Copy name.** Right-click a branch in the sidebar (local or remote) or a branch chip in the graph to copy its name. The graph's +N chip lists each branch it hides.
